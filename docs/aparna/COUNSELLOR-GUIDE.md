# Counsellor guide — a day in the CRM

**For:** the Counsellor (and the Owner). **Goal:** no enquiry is ever lost. Every family has a next step with a date.
**Address:** https://aparna-atomic-crm.vercel.app. On a phone, use your browser's menu and pick "Add to Home Screen" to install it like an app.

## Words used in the app

- **Parent** = the person you talk to on WhatsApp. **Admission** = one child's journey with us. Every enquiry is an admission.
- **Stages:** Enquiry → Trial booked → Trial done → Payment pending → Enrolled, or Lost.
- All times are India time (IST). When the parent lives abroad, their local time is shown next to IST.

## Your day, step by step

1. **Open Today** (the first screen after you sign in). Work through it from top to bottom:
   **New** (enquiries nobody has answered yet), **Follow-ups due**, **Trials today and tomorrow**, **Payment pending**, **Fees due**. Red text means late.
2. **Add an enquiry the moment it arrives.** Tap **Quick add enquiry** on Today and type the parent's name, WhatsApp number and child's name, then **Save**.
   - If the number is already in the CRM, the app attaches the new child to that parent. You will see a message saying so. Do not add the parent again.
   - Never create a parent on their own. A parent with no admission does not appear on Today, so the family would be forgotten.
3. **Send a message.** On a row, tap **WhatsApp** and pick the message, for example **Welcome**. WhatsApp opens with the text ready. Check it, then press send in WhatsApp.
   - For **Payment link**, paste the Razorpay link first, then tap **Open WhatsApp**.
   - If the button is greyed out, read the line under it. For example, the parent may have asked not to get WhatsApp messages.
4. **Set the next follow-up.** When you come back from WhatsApp, the app asks **Sent?**. Choose the date and time of the next follow-up (IST) and confirm. The message is saved as a note, and the family will come back on Today at that time.
5. **Mark a fee as paid.** Do this only after the money has arrived. Under **Fees due**, tap **Mark fee paid** and confirm. The next due date moves forward by one period. Tapping twice does not count the payment twice.
6. **Move an admission to another stage** on a computer: open **Admissions** and drag the card to the new column, or open the admission and edit it. The app asks for what that stage needs, such as the trial date and time, the fee, or the next action.
   - On a phone you can open, message and mark paid, but you cannot change a stage yet. If you need to, note the change and do it on a computer. Then tell the Owner how often this happens: it decides whether phones get stage editing.
7. **Move to Lost** when the family says no or stops replying after your last follow-up. Drag the card to **Lost** on a computer and pick the **Lost reason**. Lost families leave Today. Their history stays in the CRM.
8. **Before you finish:** **New** and **Follow-ups due** should be empty, or every row should have a new follow-up date. An enquiry with no next step is the one we lose.

## Accounts (read once)

- The CRM has exactly two staff accounts: the Owner and one Counsellor. A third person cannot be added.
- If a Counsellor leaves, the Owner disables that account in **Users** and then invites the new Counsellor. A disabled Counsellor is replaced only by a new Counsellor, never by a second person alongside.
- Teachers do not get accounts yet. They come with a later version (feature F-101, staff roles and permissions). Until then, share trial and class details with teachers outside the CRM.
- Forgot your password? On the sign-in page, tap **Forgot password?**. The email may arrive in Spam.

## When something looks wrong

Take a screenshot, write down the parent's name and what you expected, and send it to the Owner. Do not work around it by creating a second parent or admission.
